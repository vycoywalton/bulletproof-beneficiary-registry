# Bulletproof OS — North Star

## Mission

Bulletproof OS exists to help individuals discover potential life insurance beneficiary rights and preserve critical life insurance legacy information in a secure, trusted platform.

## Vision

Create the most trusted universal platform for life insurance beneficiary discovery and legacy protection.

No rightful beneficiary should miss a life insurance benefit because they did not know they were entitled to it, did not know where to search, or did not know how to begin the process.

## Phase 1 Product Focus

Bulletproof OS will initially focus on two core capabilities:

### 1. Life Insurance Beneficiary Search

Purpose:

Help individuals determine whether they may be connected to a life insurance policy or claim.

The user may:
- Not know they are a beneficiary
- Not know which insurance company holds the policy
- Not know where to begin a claim process

The platform will help organize and guide the search process.

### 2. Life Insurance Legacy Registry

Purpose:

Help individuals securely organize life insurance information so that loved ones can locate important information when needed.

The registry may include:
- Policy information
- Insurance company information
- Beneficiary information
- Important documents
- Contact information
- Legacy instructions

## Core Principles

### Trust First

Users are trusting Bulletproof OS with sensitive family and financial information. Privacy, security, and transparency are foundational.

### Start Focused

Phase 1 focuses only on life insurance. Future assets may be added after the foundation is proven.

### Universal Accessibility

The platform should be designed so it can eventually serve users across:
- Web
- Mobile devices
- Tablets
- Future platforms

### User Empowerment

Bulletproof OS helps people understand, organize, and protect their information.

## Phase 1 Boundaries

We are NOT initially building:
- All asset discovery
- Estate planning software
- Financial management tools
- Legal advice services
- Insurance underwriting systems

These may become future expansions.

## Long-Term Expansion

Future possibilities include:
- Retirement accounts
- Bank accounts
- Investments
- Real estate
- Trusts
- Digital assets
- Additional legacy information

## North Star Statement

Bulletproof OS is building a trusted life insurance beneficiary discovery and legacy protection platform that helps people find what they may be entitled to and protect what matters for future generations.
