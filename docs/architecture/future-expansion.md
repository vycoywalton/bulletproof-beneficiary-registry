# Bulletproof OS — Future Expansion Strategy

## Purpose

This document defines how Bulletproof OS can expand beyond its initial life insurance focus while maintaining a strong product foundation.

The first version focuses on life insurance beneficiary discovery and legacy registry capabilities.

Future expansion will build upon the same foundation:

People + Assets + Beneficiaries + Documents + Relationships

---

# 1. Phase 1 Foundation

## Life Insurance Beneficiary Search

Primary purpose:

Help individuals determine whether they may be connected to a life insurance policy or claim.

Core capabilities:

- Beneficiary search requests
- Identity verification
- Search tracking
- Claim organization
- Document management

---

## Life Insurance Legacy Registry

Primary purpose:

Help individuals organize life insurance information for future access.

Core capabilities:

- Policy records
- Insurance company information
- Beneficiary records
- Secure documents
- Legacy instructions

---

# 3. Universal Legacy Model

Future systems should follow a common structure:

PERSON

↓

ASSET

↓

BENEFICIARY

↓

DOCUMENTS

↓

INSTRUCTIONS

This allows different asset types to exist within the same trusted platform.

# 6. Expansion Principles

## Protect the Core Mission

Expansion should always support the mission of helping people discover and protect legacy information.

## Maintain User Trust

Every expansion must preserve:

- Privacy
- Security
- Transparency

## Build Modularly

New capabilities should be added without rebuilding the foundation.

# Future Vision

Bulletproof OS begins as a life insurance beneficiary discovery and legacy registry platform.

Over time, it can become a universal legacy operating system that helps individuals and families understand, organize, and protect the assets and information that matter most.
