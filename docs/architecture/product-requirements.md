# Bulletproof OS — Product Requirements Document

## Product Name

Bulletproof OS

## Phase 1 Product

Life Insurance Beneficiary Search & Legacy Registry

---

# 1. Problem Statement

Many individuals may be entitled to life insurance benefits but do not know they are beneficiaries, do not know where policies exist, or do not know how to begin the process of identifying and claiming benefits.

Many families also lack a secure method to organize life insurance information for future access.

Bulletproof OS addresses both problems through a trusted digital platform.

---

# 2. Primary Users

## User Type 1: Potential Beneficiary

A person who wants to determine whether they may be connected to a life insurance policy or claim.

Needs:
- A secure search process
- Identity verification
- Information organization
- Search tracking
- Claim preparation guidance

---

## User Type 2: Legacy Registry User

A person who wants to organize life insurance information for future access.

Needs:
- Secure storage
- Policy organization
- Beneficiary records
- Document management
- Legacy instructions

---

# 3. Phase 1 Core Features

## Account Creation

Users can:
- Create an account
- Verify identity
- Manage personal information
- Control privacy settings

---

## Beneficiary Search

Users can:
- Create a beneficiary search request
- Enter personal information
- Provide relationship information
- Track search progress
- Store related documents
- Record potential matches
- Track claim actions

---

## Life Insurance Legacy Registry

Users can:
- Create a life insurance record
- Add policy details
- Add insurance company information
- Store documents
- Add beneficiary information
- Record important contacts

---

# 4. User Experience Goals

The platform should be:

- Simple
- Secure
- Trustworthy
- Accessible
- Easy for non-technical users

---

# 5. Security Requirements

The platform must prioritize:

- Identity verification
- Data privacy
- Secure document storage
- Access controls
- Audit history

---

# 6. Phase 1 Non-Goals

The first version will not include:

- Other asset categories
- Investment management
- Legal document creation
- Insurance purchasing
- Financial advice

---

# 7. Future Expansion

Future versions may support:

- Retirement accounts
- Banking assets
- Investments
- Real estate
- Trusts
- Digital assets
- Family legacy planning

---

# 8. MVP Success Criteria

The first working version should allow a user to:

1. Create an account
2. Start a beneficiary search request
3. Create a life insurance legacy record
4. Store information securely
5. Track progress over time
